## Chart Reading Tips

### Heatmaps
- **Darker colors = Higher intensity/frequency**
- **White/Yellow = Low or no mentions**
- **Numbers in cells = Exact counts**
- **Read trends horizontally (across years) or vertically (within year)**

### Bar Charts
- **Value labels on/near bars for exact numbers**
- **Colors indicate categories or change direction (green=increase, red=decrease)**
- **Grouped bars allow direct comparison**
- **Horizontal bars used when many categories (easier label reading)**

### Line Charts
- **Different markers distinguish lines**
- **Area fill emphasizes magnitude**
- **Annotated points show exact values**
- **Multiple lines allow trend comparison**

### Cumulative Charts
- **Always increasing (shows total accumulated over time)**
- **Slope indicates rate of growth**
- **Steeper slope = faster growth in that period**
